\section{Linear systems}
Each equation is a row, each variable a column. For $Ax=b$, pass
\texttt{[A|b]}: m coefficients then the right-hand side.
Rank -1 means impossible; rank m means unique; otherwise free variables exist.
Choose real Gauss for measurements/expectations, modular Gauss for a prime field,
and binary Gauss for XOR constraints.
